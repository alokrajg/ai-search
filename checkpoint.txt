## 🚀 PROJECT OVERVIEW

**GEO Search Platform** is a modern, interactive web application for monitoring AI visibility and citation tracking across generative search engines. The platform provides comprehensive tools for businesses to monitor, optimize, and protect their brand presence across all major AI search platforms.

### 🎯 CORE MISSION
Help businesses navigate and thrive in the new world of AI-powered search engines by providing:
- Real-time AI visibility monitoring
- Content optimization suggestions
- Brand protection and misinformation alerts
- Comprehensive analytics and insights
